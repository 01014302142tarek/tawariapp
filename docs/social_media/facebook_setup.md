## 2. Visual Assets Checklist

Before starting, ensure you have these files ready.

### **Profile Picture**
*   **File to use:** `public/logo.png` or `public/logo-custom.png` (found in your project folder).

## 3. Step-by-Step Setup Instructions

1.  **Log in to Facebook** and go to [facebook.com/pages/create](https://www.facebook.com/pages/create).
2.  **Fill in the Basics:**
    *   Enter the **Page Name** (`TawariApp - طوارئ`).
    *   Select the **Category** (`Medical & Health`).
    *   Paste the **Short Description** into the Bio field.
    *   Click **Create Page**.
3.  **Upload Images:**
    *   Upload your **Profile Picture**.
    *   Upload your **Cover Photo**.
4.  **Add Contact Details:**
    *   Add your **Website URL**, **Email**, and **Phone Number** (optional, but recommended for trust).
    *   Set your **Location** (e.g., Cairo, Egypt) or choose "No physical location" if it's purely online.
    *   Set **Hours** to "Always Open" (since the app is available 24/7).
5.  **Add an Action Button:**
    *   Click "Add Action Button" at the top of your page.
    *   Choose **"Sign Up"** or **"Use App"**.
    *   Enter the link to your live web application.
6.  **Create Your First Post:**
    *   Write a welcome message introducing TawariApp.
    *   *Example:* "Welcome to the official TawariApp Facebook page! We are excited to bring accessible healthcare to everyone in Egypt. Stay tuned for updates! 🚀 #TawariApp #Telemedicine #Egypt"
7.  **Claim Your username:**
    *   Go to Settings -> General -> Username.
    *   Try to claim `@TawariApp`.
